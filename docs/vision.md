# Vision

## Pitch

A golf game in space where gravity is the course. You aim by pulling back like a
slingshot, planets curve your shot, and you sink the ball into a black hole. Every level
fits on one screen and can be played in under a minute.

## Goals

1. **Fun in five seconds.** No tutorial screen: the first level teaches the controls by itself.
2. **Physics you can feel.** Orbits, slingshots around planets and near misses are the game.
3. **Runs anywhere.** Desktop and mobile browsers, mouse and touch, no install, no backend.
4. **A codebase that shows how it was built.** Clear layers, tested rules, documented decisions.

## Players

- Someone who opens a link and has one minute.
- Someone who comes back to get three stars on every level.

## Scope — version 1.0

- Slingshot aiming with a short trajectory preview
- Planets with gravity and bouncy surfaces; black hole as the goal
- Strokes, par and stars (1–3) per level
- 10 hand-made levels with rising difficulty
- Best score per level saved in the browser
- Level select screen

## Non-goals (for now)

- Accounts, online leaderboards or any backend
- Level editor and shared levels (candidate for 1.1, see roadmap)
- Sound and music (candidate for 1.1)
- Realistic astrophysics: the gravity is tuned for fun, not for accuracy

## Success looks like

- A first-time player sinks level 1 without reading anything.
- 60 fps on a mid-range phone.
- Every level is provably solvable (automated test).
